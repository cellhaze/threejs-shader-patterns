Reading GLSL by feel, not just by formula

Each card below is one pattern from Bruno Simon's "Shader patterns" lesson, rebuilt so the constants in the formula — frequency, edge, offset, scale — are sliders instead of hardcoded numbers. Drag one and watch what that operation actually does to the plane.

Everything renders through a single shared WebGL context, so scrolling stays smooth even with 49 shaders on the page. Toggle Colorize in the sidebar to switch every pattern from black/white to the UV-gradient coloring covered at the end of the lesson.

Formulas adapted from Three.js Journey by Bruno Simon — Chapter 04, Lesson 28 "Shader patterns." This is a personal study reference, not a copy of the paid course; go take the actual lesson if you haven't.
